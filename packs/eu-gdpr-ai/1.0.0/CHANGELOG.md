# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-08-29

- Initial AI-oriented GDPR screening core.
- Added personal-data scope, Article 9, Article 22, Article 25 and Article 35
  rules with official EUR-Lex anchors.
- Encoded Article 22(2) conditions from constituent facts.
- Added the distinct Article 22(4) restriction for decisions based on
  special-category data, including the limited Article 9 conditions and
  suitable safeguards.
- Separated the Article 35 DPIA trigger from the gap produced when a required
  DPIA is not evidenced as complete.
