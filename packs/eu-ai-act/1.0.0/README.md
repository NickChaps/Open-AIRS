# EU AI Act core qualification pack 1.0.0

This clean-room pack implements a reviewable triage subset of Regulation (EU)
2024/1689 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744. It deliberately does not
claim full conformity coverage.

The employment rules show why the governed object is the composition and
intended use. A passive CV-screening skill can contribute to the purpose of an
application, but the skill does not call a connector and is not automatically
classified as an AI system. The final high-risk conclusion also needs the
Article 6(3) facts.

Sources reviewed: both official EUR-Lex texts and the amended application
timeline, 29 August 2026. The new Article 5 intimate-content prohibitions are
identified as a known gap rather than approximated by an incomplete rule.
