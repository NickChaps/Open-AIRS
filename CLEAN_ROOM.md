# Clean-room statement

AIR Framework is an independent implementation derived from public legal texts, public technical specifications, openly licensed materials and original analysis.

The repository must not contain:

- confidential or proprietary organisational policies;
- customer or production data;
- credentials or connection details;
- copied proprietary code;
- private contractual clauses or internal decision thresholds;
- examples that identify a real organisation or reproduce its operating rules.

Examples are fictional and designed solely to exercise the public specification. Contributors must have the right to submit every contribution and must identify material derived from third-party sources.

Legal and methodological packs should link to authoritative publications. They should not reproduce protected standards or imply endorsement by the issuing authority.
