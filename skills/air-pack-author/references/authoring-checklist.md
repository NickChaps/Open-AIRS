# Pack authoring checklist

## Source

- Official URL and stable locator
- Authority type and jurisdiction
- Source version, effective date and review date
- Lawful use and clean-room authorship confirmed

## Scope

- Applicable object types
- Coverage stated in plain language
- Known gaps and required overlays
- No certification or endorsement claim

## Rules

- Stable pack, rule, anchor and finding identifiers
- Bounded facts rather than conclusions
- Three-valued conditions preserve unknowns
- Independently written summaries
- Exact anchors for every normative result
- No organisation route embedded in a public pack

## Verification

- Positive fixture
- Negative fixture
- Unknown or conflict fixture
- Structural validation
- Full conformance suite
- Dry-run against an existing inventory
- Human review of every material diff
- Immutable version and changelog
