# Sources and coverage

| Pack | Authority | Pinned source | v0.1 coverage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EU AI Act core | Binding EU regulation | Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 | Selected prohibitions, employment high-risk route, value-chain role, literacy and interaction transparency |
| EU GDPR AI core | Binding EU regulation | Regulation (EU) 2016/679 | Selected AI processing, Article 9, Article 22, Article 25 and DPIA questions |
| NIS2 baseline | Binding directive through national law | Directive (EU) 2022/2555 | EU-level scope marker, management, selected measures and reporting process |
| NIST AI RMF | Voluntary framework | NIST AI 100-1 and AI 600-1 | Four top-level functions and GAI-profile marker |
| NIST CSF | Voluntary framework | NIST CSWP 29 | Six top-level functions |

Each `pack.json` contains the authoritative links, review date and explicit
known gaps. The NIS2 pack needs national overlays. NIST states that AI RMF 1.0
is under revision; this repository pins 1.0 until a separately reviewed pack
version is released.

ISO publications are not embedded. Their copyright and licence conditions do
not permit this repository to reproduce or operationalise protected text
without the necessary permission.
