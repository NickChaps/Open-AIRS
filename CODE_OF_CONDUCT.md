# Community conduct

Open AIRS welcomes contributors from legal, compliance, policy, security,
risk, product, research and engineering backgrounds.

Be precise without being hostile. Critique rules, evidence and reasoning rather
than people. Explain specialist terms, state uncertainty, cite sources and make
room for review by people outside your discipline. Do not publish confidential
material, personal data or allegations about identifiable organisations or
people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and deliberate misrepresentation
are not accepted. Report sensitive conduct concerns privately to the
maintainer. Until a dedicated address is published, use GitHub's private
security-advisory channel without including unnecessary personal data.

Maintainers may edit or remove material and restrict participation to protect
contributors and the integrity of the project. Decisions should be explained
and proportionate where safety and privacy permit.
